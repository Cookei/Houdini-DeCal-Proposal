- **Display Options**: Change viewport viewing parameters
- **Playbar**: Animation
# Navigation
- **Left Click**: Rotate
- **Middle Click**: Pan
- **Right Click or Scrollwheel**: Zoom

- **Space Bar**: Toggle between <u>View Mode</u> and previous tool

- **View Mode**:
	- **1**: Perspective Mode
	- **2**: Top View
	- **3**: Front View
	- **4**: Right View
	- **5**: UV View
Double click to do the opposite. IE: tapping 2 twice → bottom view
Can also switch views in upper right hand corner of viewport
