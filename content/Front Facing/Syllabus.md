# Course Description and Structure
This course is intended for students who are interested in learning VFX and proceduralism for 3d cg workflows, with a primarily focus for film. We will cover topics such as [[Front Facing/Topics/Procedural Modeling]], [[Front Facing/Topics/VOP\|VOPs]], [[Front Facing/Topics/COP\|COPs]], Simulations, and much more.
- Students will work individually on homework assignments, incorporating what they learned in lecture. Projects will be more involved assignments that are meant to be more challenging. Additionally, there will be a final project where they will submit a demo reel showcase of their choosing.
- Every class will primarily consist of lecture demonstrating various tools and concepts used in Houdini as a demo follow along.
## Enrollment and Prerequisites
Enrollment will be based on an application which involves the submission of an initial homework assignment along with a form containing relevant information about the student. The details of the assignment will be given at the introductory lecture during the infosession.

It is highly recommended that the student has prerequisite knowledge of cg workflows for modeling and animation, including but not limited to: modeling, uv unwrapping, shading, rendering. **UCBUGG Basic**, **CS 184**, **ART 172**, or equivalent knowledge should be sufficient.

Additionally, it is recommended but not required to have **MATH 54** or **EECS 16a** equivalent knowledge on linear algebra, as well as a cursory understanding of the logical principles that govern the thought processes behind programming.

Students are also highly recommended to have access to a decent functioning computer that can run Houdini, as well as a mouse. However, this is not a hard requirement as key card access to the computer lab will be granted after registration.

# Assignments
## Attendance
At the end of every class period, there will be an attendance form to fill out.
## Homework
Homeworks are quick and creative. Students will be encouraged to explore some of the topics covered in lecture to any degree they desire. Homeworks will be submitted electronically on Gradescope. Please follow the [[Homework Submission Guidelines]].

Additionally, your lowest 2 homework assignments will be dropped.
## Projects
Projects are more involved and longer. Students are encouraged to start early. Projects will be submitted on Gradescope. Please follow the [[Homework Submission Guidelines]]. Projects are meant to be more structured deeper dives into specific systems in Houdini.
### Final Project
The [[Front Facing/Assignments/Projects/Final Project\|Final Project]] cannot be extended. Students will have approximately 1 month to work on the final project, which first consists of a proposal. Once approved, students will work on the final project until the end of class, where there will be a group showcase.
## Extensions
There is a google form for requesting extensions. Students can request 1 week extensions for any assignment, no questions asked, with no limit. However, if we notice that you are abusing extension requests, we reserve the right to deny extension requests.

For attendance, there is also an excused absence form, no questions asked. There is a maximum of 2 excused absences and 1 unexcused absence.

For any extenuating circumstances, please contact the staff at ucbugg+houdini@gmail.com.
## Late Policy
Homework that are turned in late will be deducted 10% per day late, up to a maximum of 50%. The final project cannot be turned in late.

There is a maximum of 2 excused absences and 1 unexcused absence before a 1% penalty is deducted per absence on the final grade.

# Auditing
Students are free to audit the course. They may request to be added to the course Gradescope to have their homework graded, and can attend office hours just as normal students. However, priority will be given to those officially enrolled. Auditors' Final Projects will also not be showcased at the end of semester showcase.
